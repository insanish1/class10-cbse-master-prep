# Question-bank refresh: 20 September 2026

## Scope
Fresh Class 10 practice items added across Mathematics, Science, Social Science, English, French and Artificial Intelligence 417. Existing app data shape is preserved: `text`, `options`, `answer`, `type`, `marks`, `chapter`, `explanation`, with additional `difficulty` and `competency` metadata.

## Official curriculum/assessment checks
- CBSE 2026-27 curriculum release was checked through the official CBSE Academic circular listing (Acad-14/2026).
- CBSE Artificial Intelligence 417 Class X 2026-27 curriculum was checked directly. The current structure includes Revisiting AI Project Cycle & Ethical Frameworks, Advanced Concepts of Modeling, Evaluating Models, Statistical Data, Computer Vision, NLP and Advance Python. The new AI questions reflect these current units.
- CBSE official SQP/marking material was checked for assessment style. The latest directly surfaced official subject materials include Mathematics Standard and Social Science 2025-26 SQP/marking schemes and French 2025-26 SQP. These were used for question-type/competency style where a 2026-27 subject-specific SQP was not surfaced.
- NCERT official textbook resources and exemplar resources were checked as the curriculum/content reference layer.

## Refresh policy
Questions are new rather than copies of the original starter set. The bank intentionally mixes application, reasoning, case-based, numerical, reading, writing and literature/grammar items where supported by the current app schema.

## Important deployment note
This refresh expands the bank but does **not** yet make the full-mock engine a complete 80-mark/board-length paper generator. The current app still selects from the available bank and therefore remains a development-stage test suite until the larger question-bank and paper-blueprint engine are implemented.
